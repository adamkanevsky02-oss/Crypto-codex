## Already closed for 2027 — do not waste time

US and EMEA bulge-bracket summer analyst cycles (Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan,
Citi, BofA, Morgan Stanley, Barclays, Deutsche Bank, UBS) opened and closed
between **December 2025 and January 2026**. Those classes were filled by
May 2026.

**This does not necessarily apply to their Hong Kong / APAC full-time intakes**,
which run on separate and often later timelines — HSBC's HK deadline above is
proof. Check each firm's APAC careers page directly rather than assuming the
US date applies.

## What this means for strategy

The formal grad-scheme door for 2027 is **partly closed and closing further**.
That makes cold outreach to startups and mid-size AI-finance firms the higher-value
channel, not the fallback. Those companies hire when they meet someone good,
not on a calendar.
